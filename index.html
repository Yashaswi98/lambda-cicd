Chinmaya Vidyalaya

Chinmaya Vidyalaya is spread over 1.5 acres of land. It is situated in a serene, scenic and ECO friendly environment. It has spacious and well ventilated class rooms.

It also provides playgrounds, garden, indoor/outdoor games, advanced computer labs with leased line internet facility, well stocked library with well researched study materials, well equipped Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Biology Lab, Math Lab, Social Lab Audio Visual Room, with latest digi boards. School also provides transport.
